OpenSSL Third-Party Notice (Windows managed Python runtime)
==========================================================

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

The SoAI Windows distribution includes a pinned Python 3.13 archive from Astral's Python Build Standalone project
and installs it into the managed runtime when needed. The archive contains OpenSSL libraries, including
`DLLs/libssl-3-x64.dll` and `DLLs/libcrypto-3-x64.dll`, and retains their terms in its upstream `LICENSE.txt`.
