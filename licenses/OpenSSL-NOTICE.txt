OpenSSL Third-Party Notice (Windows managed Python runtime)
==========================================================

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

The SoAI Windows installer downloads the pinned Python 3.13 NuGet runtime into the SoAI installation. That runtime
contains OpenSSL libraries, including `libssl-3.dll` and `libcrypto-3.dll`, and retains its upstream `LICENSE.txt`.
